## Section 2: Must-Run and Heat-Led Plants

The following plants are primarily dispatched by heat demand or industrial steam
contracts, not electricity price. Inclusion in the merit order is an approximation;
these plants are unlikely to set the marginal price.

### Industrial CHP (steam-driven dispatch)
- **Elsta Cogeneration**: Heat-led CHP supplying steam to Dow Chemical Hoek van Holland (~500 t/h steam, 90 MW electrical to Dow). Dispatch driven by industrial steam demand, not electricity price. Included in merit order as approximation.
- **Moerdijk 1 CHP**: Heat-led CHP, Shell Moerdijk chemical site. Dispatch driven by industrial steam demand.
- **Rijnmond 1**: Heat-led CHP, industrial dispatch not purely price-driven.
- **Delesto 2**: Industrial CHP, Chemiepark Delfzijl (Akzo Nobel/Nobian). Heat-led dispatch.
- **NAM Schoonebeek**: Oil field steam generation for NAM Schoonebeek heavy oil extraction. Operationally driven, not price-driven.
- **Pergen 1/2**: Industrial CHP, Shell Moerdijk / Air Liquide Pernis. Heat-led dispatch.
- **Swentibold Chemelot**: Industrial CHP, Chemelot industrial park. Heat-led dispatch. 300 t/h steam at 140 bar/525C and 18 bar/300C.
- **Velsen 24**: Same as Velsen 25. See paper Section 5.

### District heating CHP
- **Diemen 33 CHP**: District heating Amsterdam. Heat-led dispatch.
- **Lage Weide 6**: District heating Utrecht. Heat-led dispatch.
- **Merwedekanaal Pegus 12**: District heating Utrecht. Heat-led dispatch.
- **Den Haag Power station**: District heating The Hague. Heat-led dispatch.
- **RoCa**: District heating Rotterdam area. Heat-led dispatch.

### Steelworks waste-gas plants (Tata Steel Velsen)
- **Velsen 25**: Operationally driven by Tata Steel IJmuiden steelworks production. BFG is a steelworks by-product. Exact fuel mix (BFG + coke oven gas + natural gas support) not publicly disclosed. Treated as merit-order participant following Schram et al. (2019) methodology. See paper Section 5.

### Moerdijk 1 CHP - operational status uncertainty
Mothballed February 2018 (EEX Transparency [30]). Some generation in 2021.
Status uncertain for 2022-2025. Capacity set to 0 MW in historical CSVs from 2018.
Verify against ENTSO-E Transparency Platform for sensitivity analysis.

### Sloecentrale 10/20 - upgrade note
Operator confirmed 58.7% [9]. EP Power Europe reports >60% after 2024-2025 upgrade.
Historical CSVs 2017-2023: 0.587; 2024-2025: 0.600. Main meritorder_NL.csv: 0.600.

### Maasstroom Rijnmond 2 - efficiency revised 0.590 -> 0.520 (PRIMARY)
Single-shaft SGT5-4000F + SST5-3000 operates at lower net efficiency than multi-shaft
configurations at Sloecentrale or Enecogen. This shifts Maasstroom further up the merit
order (higher SRMC). Source: Power Technology [14].

### BFG emission factor (Velsen 24 and 25)
Model uses Schram et al. (2019) [17] methodology: BFG EF = gas EF x 1.25
= 0.20232 x 1.25 = **0.25290 kg CO2/kWh_th** (market.py THERMAL_CO2).
Velsen fuel purchase cost = 0 EUR/MWh_th (steelworks waste gas).
SRMC = CO2 cost + var_OM only.

### CHP electrical efficiency ambiguity
ESTIMATE plants use OPSD values that likely reflect total CHP fuel utilization (electrical
+ heat), not electrical-only efficiency. This may overstate merit-order competitiveness.
Stated as a limitation in paper Section 5.
